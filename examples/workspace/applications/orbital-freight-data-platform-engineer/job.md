## About Orbital Freight

Orbital Freight runs a load-matching marketplace for European road freight. Around 600
people across Leeds, Rotterdam and Warsaw. Profitable since 2024.

## The role

The Data Platform team (six engineers) owns ingestion, the lakehouse and the serving layer
behind pricing and matching. We are moving from a Databricks-centric batch platform to a
mixed batch and streaming architecture on Iceberg, and we need an engineer who has done
this before.

## Responsibilities

- Own ingestion from the marketplace event bus and from carrier telematics partners.
- Build and maintain streaming pipelines feeding the pricing model's feature store.
- Drive the Iceberg migration for the three largest domains.
- Improve the platform's observability — we currently find out about failures from Slack.

## Requirements

- 5+ years in data engineering, at least two on a platform team.
- Strong Python, SQL and Spark.
- Production Kafka. Streaming compute experience preferred but not required if the Kafka
  depth is there.
- Iceberg, Delta or Hudi at scale.
- Comfortable with Terraform and CI/CD ownership.
