## Primary track

Platform-build data engineering at a company that is still building, not maintaining:
someone who owns pipeline architecture, gets to make calls, and has streaming or
near-real-time in the remit. The record supports this. Nine years, current ownership of a
400-person retailer's analytics platform, a real cost-and-runtime track record, and one
production Kafka feed. Most applications go here.

## Secondary track

Near-real-time analytics platform roles that are streaming-adjacent but do not require a
production stream-processing track record yet: teams moving off batch where the Kafka
transport experience plus the platform ownership is enough to get in and grow. This is the
stretch, and it is where the gap below gets closed on the job rather than before it.

## The gap

- **Production stream processing (Flink or Spark Streaming serving live traffic)** —
  closeable, and best closed inside a role. One Kafka transport layer is not the same as
  owning a streaming system. A bridge job on the secondary track, or a first year in a
  primary-track role where streaming is "coming", closes it in 12 to 18 months. A side
  project would help the story but will not substitute.
- **Operating Kubernetes** — closeable and smaller. Can read a manifest, has deployed to a
  cluster someone else ran, has never operated one. A focused month plus asking for cluster
  work in the current role covers most of it. Not a reason to skip a posting that lists it.
- **No recognisable-brand employer, one company for five years** — not a skills gap and not
  fixable by upskilling. It is a framing question for the résumé and the interview, not a
  target change.

## Calibration

About right, with one notch of stretch on the secondary track. The record clears the bar
for senior platform roles; the specialist streaming roles are a level up on that one axis
and should be a minority of applications until the gap closes.

## Sustainability check

The visa runs to August 2028 and any employer must hold a sponsor licence and be willing
to use it. That check comes before role quality, every time. Below a 75k base a move is not
worth the disruption and the visa risk, so those postings do not get evenings spent on
them regardless of how good the work looks. This is why Kestrel Labs (sponsors, streaming
platform, strong match) is live, Trellis Bio (cannot sponsor) was closed at an 80 percent
paper match, and Orbital Freight is on hold until the sponsor-register check is run.
