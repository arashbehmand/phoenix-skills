## About Trellis Bio

Trellis Bio is a computational biology company working on protein design for agricultural
applications. Seventy people, Series A, spun out of a university lab in 2022.

## The role

You will own the analytics layer that the research and commercial teams depend on. This is
a dbt-centric role: most of your time will be spent modelling, testing and documenting in
a Snowflake and dbt stack, working closely with scientists to turn experimental data into
something the rest of the business can use.

## Responsibilities

- Own and extend a dbt project of roughly 400 models.
- Partner with the research team to model experimental output.
- Build and maintain dashboards for the commercial team.
- Improve documentation and test coverage across the warehouse.

## Requirements

- 3+ years in analytics engineering or a similar role.
- Expert dbt and SQL.
- Snowflake.
- Comfortable working directly with non-technical stakeholders.
- Python for light scripting.

## Reporting line

This role reports into the Finance function.
